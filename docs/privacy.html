Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Nexa Tap is a digital business card app. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo.
  • Card details you add: job title, company, phone numbers, links, social profiles, bio and photos.
  • Orders: the delivery address and phone number you give when ordering a physical card.
  • Profile views: when someone opens your card we count the visit and whether it came from NFC, QR or a link. We do not record visitors' identity.
  • Shared contacts: if a visitor chooses "Share my contact" on your profile, the name, phone, email and note they type are sent to you.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We never see your Google password and we do not access any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show your digital card to people you share it with.
  • To process and deliver card orders.
  • To show you view statistics and notifications.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

What is public

The details you add to a card are visible to anyone who opens that card's link, QR code or NFC tag. You can turn a card off at any time in the app to hide it.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with our hosting provider, Supabase. Access is protected so that only you can edit your own data.

Deleting your data

You can edit or remove card details in the app at any time. To delete your account and all its data, email us from your account email and we will delete it within 30 days.
